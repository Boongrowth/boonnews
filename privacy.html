Data Compliance & Security
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 Privacy Verified

At BoonNews, accessible from https://boonnews.vercel.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by BoonNews and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you browse BoonNews, subscribe to our newsletters, or interact with our platform, we may collect information directly from you or automatically via standard web technologies:

  • Personal Information: Email addresses provided voluntarily when subscribing to our newsletter or contacting our team.
  • Log Data: Standard server access logs including IP addresses, browser types, Internet Service Providers (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts.
  • Local Storage: Site preferences such as bookmarked articles stored locally within your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected data for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Providing, operating, and maintaining our news platform.
  • Improving, personalizing, and expanding site performance and layout.
  • Understanding and analyzing reader traffic trends and usage patterns.
  • Sending daily or weekly email newsletters (only if opted-in).
  • Preventing fraudulent behavior and enhancing cyber security.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, BoonNews uses 'cookies'. These cookies store information including visitors' preferences and page views to optimize reader experience by customizing content based on browser types or other information.

"We respect your digital privacy. BoonNews will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers."

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

We rely on trusted services to run our news operations smoothly, including web analytics for understanding reader engagement. These third-party vendors may collect non-personally identifiable IP data according to their own privacy practices.

5. Children's Information

Protecting children's privacy online is paramount. BoonNews does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove it.

6. Your Rights & Data Protection

Depending on your location (such as under GDPR or CCPA), you have the right to request access to your data, request deletion of your personal email from newsletter lists, or restrict certain data processing activities.

7. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact our legal compliance team at privacy@boonnews.com.
