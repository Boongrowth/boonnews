Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Boon News (accessible via boonnewsng.blog). We respect your personal privacy and are committed to protecting it through complete transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect when you visit our publication, how we utilize that data, and the measures we take to safeguard your information.

By accessing or using our website, you signify your assent to the practices described in this document.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better editorial services, improve speed and user experience, and analyze audience interaction. This includes:

  • Log Files & Technical Data: Like standard web platforms, Boon News uses log files. Data includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser brand/version, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. This data is non-personally identifiable.
  • Voluntary Personal Data: When you subscribe to our newsletter, submit news tips, or fill out our Contact form, we may collect your name, email address, and message contents.

3. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Boon News serves advertisements delivered by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on users' prior visits to our site or other websites on the Internet.

Notice Regarding Google DART Cookies:
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to Boon News and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics Networks

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Boon News. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used by third-party ad networks to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize ad content.

Please note that Boon News has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Union)

We want to ensure you are fully aware of all of your data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Every user is entitled to the following:

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict processing of your personal data.

6. CCPA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have specific rights regarding their personal information:

  • The right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about consumers.
  • The right to request that a business delete any personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Do Not Sell My Personal Information: Boon News does not sell user personal information to third parties.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Protecting children's privacy online is paramount. Boon News does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Contact Information

If you have questions, feedback, or require more information regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out directly:

  • Email: contact@boonnewsng.blog
